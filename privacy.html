Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

SmashPad is a personal project operated by Sidharth Mittal, an individual based in India. Protecting children's privacy matters to us, so we built SmashPad to collect as little as possible — effectively nothing.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information. SmashPad has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not know who you are and we do not build any profile of you or your child.

What stays on your device

SmashPad saves a couple of harmless preferences (such as whether sounds are on or off) in your browser's local storage, on your device only. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Payments (Razorpay)

If — and only if — you choose to make a voluntary contribution, the payment is handled by Razorpay, our payment processor. When you open the payment window, any details you enter (such as UPI ID or card information) go directly to Razorpay and are handled under Razorpay's privacy policy. We never see or store your payment details. The Razorpay button/script loads only in connection with the Support feature.

Children's privacy

SmashPad is designed to be safe for young children. Because we collect no personal data and require no account, there is nothing for a child to disclose. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
